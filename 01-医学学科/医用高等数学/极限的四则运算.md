#医用高等数学

# 计算法则

1. $\lim_{x \to x_0} [f(x)+g(x)]= \lim_{x \to x_0} f(x) + \lim_{x \to x_0} g(x)$
2. $\lim_{x \to x_0} [f(x) \cdot g(x)]= \lim_{x \to x_0} f(x) \cdot \lim_{x \to x_0} g(x)$
3. $\lim_{x \to x_0} \frac{f(x)}{g(x)}=\frac{\lim_{x \to x_0} f(x)}{\lim_{x \to x_0} g(x)} (\lim_{x \to x_0} g(x)≠0)$
	1. $\lim_{x \to x_0} [f_1(x_1)+f_2(x)+……+f{X_0}(x_)]= \lim_{x \to x_0} f_1(x) + \lim_{x \to x_0} f_2(x)  +……+ \lim_{x \to x_0} f_{x_0}(x)$ (只有项数为有限个的时候才一定成立)
	2. $\lim_{x \to x_0} [f_1(x_1) \cdot f_2(x) \cdot …… \cdot f{X_0}(x_)]= \lim_{x \to x_0} f_1(x) \cdot \lim_{x \to x_0} f_2(x) \cdot …… \cdot \lim_{x \to x_0} f_{x_0}(x)$ (只有项数为有限个的时候才一定成立)
4. $\lim_{x \to x_0}[f(x)]^n=[\lim_{x \to x_0}f(x)]^n$
5. $\lim_{x \to x_0}[f(kx)]=k\lim_{x \to x_0}f(x)$
6. $\lim_{x \to x_0}f(x)=f(\lim_{x \to x_0} x)$
7. $\lim_{x \to x_0} e^{\frac{x}{x+1}}=e^{\lim_{x \to x_0} \frac{x}{x+1}}$
8. $\lim_{x \to x_0} \sqrt{x^2-2}=\sqrt{\lim_{x \to x_0}(x^2-2)}$

# 两个重要极限

1. $\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin x}{x}=1 \Rightarrow \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\tan x}{x}$
2. $\lim_{x \to \infty} (1+ \frac{1}{x})^x=e \Rightarrow \lim_{x \to \infty} (1+ x)^{ \frac{1}{x}}$

# 几个技巧题

1. 消去零因子法
	$\lim_{x \to -1} \frac{x^2 -x-2}{x+1}=\lim_{x \to -1} \frac{(x-2)(x+1)}{x+1}=\lim_{x \to -1} (x-2)=-3$
	$\lim_{x \to -1} \frac{1}{x+1}-\frac{3}{x^3+1}=\lim_{x \to -1} \frac{(x+1)(x-2)}{(x+1)(x^2-x+1)}=\lim_{x \to -1} \frac{x-2}{x^2-x+1}= \frac{-1-2}{1+1+1}=-1$
2. 易错知识点：
	- $\lim_{x \to x_0}f(x)=A \iff \lim_{x \to x_0^-}f(x)=\lim_{x \to x_0^-}f(x)=A$
	$\because \lim_{x \to x_0^+}f(x)=-1,\lim_{x \to x_0^-}f(x)=1$
	$\therefore \lim_{x \to x_0}f(x)不存在$
3. 遇到反三角函数
	1. 令$\arctan x=t,则x=\tan t$
	2. 令$\arcsin x=t,则x=\sin t$
	3. 令$\arccos x=t,则x=\cos t$
	4. 令$arccot x=t,则x=\cot t$
	e.g.  $\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\arctan x}{x}$,令$\arctan x=t,则x=\tan t$,且$t \to 0$
	原式=$\lim_{t \to 0} \frac{t}{\tan t}= \frac{1}{\lim_{t \to 0} \frac{\tan t}{t}}$=1
