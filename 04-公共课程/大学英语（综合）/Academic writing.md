# content

1. introduction
	1. function
		1. to atract interest
		2. provede some background information
		3. propose the claim
	2. component
		1. general statement
			1. funnel introduction
			2. attention-getting introduction
				1. use adecdote, interesting story, incident
				2. raise questions
				3. use a quotation
		2. thesis statement
			1. intorduce the main point
			2. then introduce sub-topic 1,2,3……
		3. key impacts
			1. specific: chose a broad topic, then narrow it down
			2. precision: remain focused on the topic
			3. demonstrability
2. main body(each paragraph)
	1. topic sentence
		1. supporting the thesis statement
		2. indicate the key idea
		3. relate back or forward
	2. supporting idea
		1. reasons
		2. examples
		3. facts
	3. conclusion sentences
3. inclusion
	1. summary
	2. final comment
	3. components
		1. comparisions with other studies
		2. summary of the main body\
		3. limitations of research
		4. suggestions for the further research
		5. practical implications and proposals
