# thesis：life

# topic：excessive houseplants can cause anxiety

# 生词：

1. adorn v. 装饰，装扮(=decorate)
2. dose n. 一次，（药的）一份；剂量，服用量
3. reap v,收获，取得（成果）
4. utilize = use
5. increment n.增长，增额
6. intensify v.(= increase)

# 演讲稿

There is an increasingly trend  that people adorn their homes  with greenery. Researches have suggest that houseplants boost our mental health, the ability of cognition and reduce the possibility to get sick.
But what dose of indoor greenery is ideal to reap the most benefits?
For a precise measurement, researchers from the Stanford University utilize the Nature View Potential tool to change the light indoor to stimulate the variation of the dose of greenery.By survey the participants in different tasks, including counting down from 1022 to zero in increments of 13.The research indicated that a 20 percent dose of  nature was the best,yet the 60 percent dose of nature could intensify stress most.
Over all, the research experiment conducted  by Stanford University showcased that the view that more houseplants, more beneficial is wrong.
